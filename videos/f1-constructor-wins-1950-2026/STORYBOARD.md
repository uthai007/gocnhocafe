# F1 constructors by cumulative wins, 1950 → 2026 — storyboard v1

**Format:** 16:9, 1920×1080, ~2:34, 13 beats. Music only, no voiceover; every line on screen. No captions, so the full frame is usable.

**Spine:** one continuous bar race. Top 10 constructors, ranked by total Grand Prix wins, in team colours, with the axis
rescaling as totals climb. A race-by-race counter and a season dial sit top right. Every other beat is the same chart,
slowed down or held. The film never cuts away to a new card until the final table.

**Truthfulness:** every number comes from F1DB (1,166 races, 13 May 1950 – 11 Oct 2026, Singapore GP 2026 = round 17 of 23).
Driver names in callouts are the race winners recorded in F1DB. No team logos (trademarks); names, colours, nationality flags.

**Look:** carbon black #0e0e11 with a faint grid, F1 red #e10600 as the only UI accent, team colours on bars only.
Type: League Gothic (display, uppercase), Oswald (labels and numbers, tabular), Inter (small print).

**Bans:** no glow, no 3D cars, no fake TV graphics package, no logo marks, no slideshow of separate cards mid-race.
Motion failures to avoid: the screensaver (bars drifting with nothing said for 20 s) and the strobe (ranks flickering at full speed).

**Held frames:** Beat 7 (Adelaide 1993) and Beat 13 (final table) stop the clock while the line lands.

**Pace:** about 1.6 s per season by default. The race slows to roughly a quarter of that speed through each lead change, so the overtake is readable.
