PTW Semi Market Brief for Fri 16 Oct 2026 (SGT). (Full text as supplied by the owner.)
While memory stocks were still digesting Tuesday's AI scare, TSMC put up another record quarter and said it will chase every last wafer of AI demand — including a look at Texas and a customer's Terafab. Applied Materials, meanwhile, opened the doors on a US$5bn collaborative R&D cleanroom campus in Sunnyvale built to shrink lab-to-fab cycles.
Japan — Quiet today.
Korea — Samsung at GMIF2026 (15 Oct, Shenzhen): V10 BV-NAND, industry's first 400+ layer V-NAND: +58% bit density, +33% I/O, −25% power vs V9; PCIe Gen6 SSD PM1763 (samples Q4 2026); zNAND-O for on-device AI (UCIe, ~10x DRAM bit density). SK hynix x US NAND / Dalian tools — Reuters (15 Oct): Solidigm/SK hynix seeking US approval to install more US-made tools in unused Mod B space at Dalian, China, linked to talks on a possible US NAND fab (upstate New York a leading site). Company says it is not pursuing China capacity expansion.
Taiwan — TSMC Q3 (15 Oct): revenue NT$1.494tn (~US$46.7bn, +50.9% YoY), net income NT$763bn (+68.7%), EPS NT$29.43, gross margin 66.4%. Advanced nodes (>=7nm) 79% of wafer revenue; 2nm 8%. FY2026 growth guide ~44% USD; Q4 revenue US$52.3–54.3bn. One more 3nm plant in Tainan, volume 1H 2028; Arizona Fab 2 construction complete, tool install under way, mass production Q3 2027 (slightly ahead). 2nm capacity to more than double next year. CEO C.C. Wei: evaluating capacity beyond Arizona incl. Texas; talks with a customer building Terafab — fab must be run by TSMC; no deal yet.
China — Quiet today. India — Quiet today.
South East Asia — FIC Global PRO3C grand opening of i-Park @ Senai Airport City plant, Johor (15 Oct): SMT for 800G–3.2T optical transceivers, CPO/LPO, silicon photonics for AI/HPC; planned Singapore Innovation & Supply Chain Centre (Johor–Singapore twinning).
Europe — GF Dresden SPRINT (9 Oct): topping-out of EUR1.1bn expansion; >1m 300mm wpy by end-2028; FDX Fusion next-gen FD-SOI platform for Physical AI planned for Dresden (IPCEI AST, pending German approval).
USA — Applied Materials EPIC Center (12 Oct, Sunnyvale): US$5bn, ~600k sq ft, nearly four acres of cleanroom; world's largest collaborative chip process/equipment R&D facility; founding partners TSMC, Intel, Samsung, Micron, SK hynix. SEMI (SEMICON West, 13 Oct): 300mm front-end fab equipment spend US$149bn in 2026 (+31% YoY), US$227bn by 2029; 20 new 300mm fabs/lines start construction in 2026 (3 in US).
